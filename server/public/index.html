FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a new number?

No. Use the SIM you already have, or forward your existing number to the phone running VaaniFlow.

Will the AI answer my personal calls?

Not if you don't want it to. Saved contacts ring normally, you can pick which SIM the AI handles, and the phone can ring for you first before the AI steps in.

Which languages does it speak?

Hindi, Hinglish and English out of the box, replying in the language the customer uses.

Does WhatsApp automation need the WhatsApp Business API?

No. You link your WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number with a QR code, like WhatsApp Web. Keep messaging natural; WhatsApp may restrict numbers used for bulk or unsolicited messages.

Where does my data live?

On your own server: a VPS, Azure, AWS or any machine that runs Docker or Node.js.
